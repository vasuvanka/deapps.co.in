Generous Negative Space

Unbroken dark fields prioritize breathing room and focus over decorative filler. Pure blacks are avoided in favor of deep obsidian (#0D0D11) to prevent smearing on OLED panels while keeping contrast calm.

Architectural Typography

Sharp structural hierarchy replaces colorful icon grids. Scale and weight variation (Plus Jakarta Sans Light 300 through Semi-Bold 600) and JetBrains Mono define interface structure.

Micro-Tactile Definition

Razor-thin low-contrast outlines (#23222B) and warm champagne interaction cues provide grounding without heavy dropped shadows. Touch press triggers instantaneous 0ms scale transitions.

deApps Zero-Telemetry Privacy Charter

1. Absolute Zero Telemetry: None of our applications (OpenPause, deLauncher, deMusic) embed tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, Firebase analytics, crash dump aggregators, or telemetry reporting tools.

2. Local-First Storage: Your app usage stats, impulse wins history, search queries, and settings are stored entirely in local on-device SQLite / Preferences. Your data never touches a server.

3. Required Permissions:
• OpenPause: Requires Accessibility & Usage Stats solely to detect when configured addictive apps are launched on your local phone.
• deLauncher: Requires Default Home / Launcher permissions to display your home screen and dock.

4. Verification: Our network manifests contain zero hidden background network communication endpoints. You can audit or monitor with any network proxy tool.
